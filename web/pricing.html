Questions

Pricing FAQ

So what does it cost?
Nothing. The source is MIT licensed and the only thing you spend is the time to run a broker and, for cross-network sessions, a TURN server. No price has been set for anything else because nothing else is being sold yet.
Is the free tier crippled in any way?
No. Every security and session feature is in the source, under the MIT licence. What you take on is running a broker and a TURN server, and being your own support desk.
Is there a free trial?
There is no trial, because there is no paid product to trial from. Self-hosting is the product, and it is free permanently under the licence it already carries.
Can I buy support?
Not today. There is no support contract, no response-time commitment and no sales process. Questions and bugs go to the issue tracker, where the answers stay public.
If you host a broker one day, will it see my screen?
No. Who operates the signaling service does not change what it can see: media is peer-to-peer and encrypted end to end, and a relay only forwards packets it has no key for. That property comes from the protocol, not from the hosting arrangement.

It is free, so start there

Build it, read the source, run your own broker. If a managed service ever appears, nothing you do now will be locked out of it.
